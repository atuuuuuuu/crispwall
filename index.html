Make any picture a sharp wallpaper for your screen.

Pick a photo. Crispwall sizes it for your exact phone or computer and uses AI to fill in the detail a small picture is missing. The AI runs on your own device, so the picture never leaves it.

How to set it as your wallpaper

iPhone: tap Share or save to Photos, then Save Image. Open Photos, tap the picture, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.

Android: download it, open it in Photos or Gallery, tap the menu and choose Set as wallpaper.

Mac: download it, then right-click the file in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture.

Windows: download it, then right-click the file and choose Set as desktop background.

Why your wallpapers look blurry

A phone or laptop screen has more pixels than most pictures online. Set a 1000-pixel picture on a 2560-pixel MacBook screen and the computer stretches it, and stretching makes it soft. Crispwall uses an AI model called Real-ESRGAN that was trained to rebuild edges and texture, then resizes the result to the exact pixel size of your screen.

Nothing is uploaded

Most "free" upscalers send your photo to their server and limit you to a few a day. Crispwall downloads a 5 MB model once and runs it in your browser, on your graphics chip when it can. The page isn't allowed to send data anywhere: its security policy blocks every connection except to this site. No account, no watermark, no limit.

When it can't help

The AI can sharpen and clean up, but it can't invent a face that isn't there. Very small pictures (less than a quarter of your screen's width) will still look soft, and Crispwall tells you when that's the case.
